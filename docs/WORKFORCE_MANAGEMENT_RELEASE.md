# Workforce and management delivery

This delivery adds working staffing and manager workflows after Phase 2. It does **not** declare the entire competitive roadmap complete. R3–R5, the delegated approval matrix and the remaining acceptance criteria stay in `IMPLEMENTATION_TRACKER.md`.

## Staff workflows

- `/suite/workforce/`: date-filtered duty board, staff presence, clinician workload/capacity, department coverage attention and pending handovers. Times use the configured `TIME_ZONE` (Africa/Kampala). Presence is based on recorded clock events, never on login.
- Managers create facility/department shifts with assigned staff, supervisor, optional backup, on-call marker and capacity. Weekly recurrence creates up to 12 **drafts**, each requiring review/publication. Overnight shifts are supported; an individual shift is limited to 24 hours. Published overlapping duties and approved leave are rejected. Cancellation preserves history and requires a reason; worked shifts cannot be cancelled. Published shifts are corrected by cancellation/replacement or an approved cover request, not by rewriting their schedule.
- Staff clock into their own current published duty, start/end breaks, clock out and explicitly choose whether they accept patients. Replays do not duplicate attendance or open breaks. Breaks and clock-out clear availability. Staff cannot clock for colleagues. Attendance is not proof of location or a biometric assertion.
- Reception/nurse/clinician/admin duty-board assignment checks active doctor, current duty, open attendance, no active break, accepting status and open-visit capacity. Assignment records the previous clinician and reason. Existing clinical visit-claim routes remain available; this release does not impose a mandatory roster on every legacy consultation route.
- Leave is private to the staff member and managers. Another supervisor reviews it; a published duty must first be cancelled or covered before overlapping leave can be approved. Future replacement/cover arrangements require the replacement's acceptance, matching role and independent supervisor approval. Reciprocal swaps can be arranged as two reviewed cover requests; there is no automatic two-way swap transaction.
- Attendance corrections preserve original clock events and record requested effective times, break minutes, reason and reviewer. Another supervisor approves them; corrected intervals cannot overlap another attendance. Approval resets prior timesheet review. Close a live attendance before requesting a correction. Exact late/time-beyond-scheduled-end values do not authorize overtime or apply payroll rounding.
- `/suite/workforce/timesheets/`: own attendance for staff, facility-scoped review for managers, 1–90 day filter and approved CSV export. Items with pending corrections are excluded from approved export. Original times remain visible beside effective times.
- Outgoing shift holders write operational handovers to another colleague in the same role. The recipient acknowledges responsibility. Pending/overdue acknowledgments are visible; clinical and HR source content must stay in its restricted records.
- `/suite/workforce/directory/`: manager-only staff directory and credential review/expiry register. Active account status is shown; it is not a full employment-contract record. Credential entries are staff-reviewed evidence, not live licensing authority verification. Review renewal entries alongside older expired records.

## Manager workflows

- `/suite/management/`: facility-scoped exception counts with links to cases, corrective actions, equipment, checklists, expense approvals, staffing, existing tasks, stock and finance.
- Restricted incidents/complaints have severity, accountable manager, due date, corrective actions, evidence, resolution and independent closure review. Reopening is audited. Managers own corrective actions because the register may contain sensitive information.
- Assets have facility-unique tags, serial numbers, location, custodian and service/calibration dates. Append-only service events record vendor, cost, evidence and next date. Downtime, explicit return-to-service and retirement are separate events; maintenance alone does not declare equipment operational. Recorded service cost is not automatically a paid expense or a clinical QC result.
- Staff onboarding, offboarding, training and policy checklist items have an owner, due date, instructions, document/version reference, completion evidence, staff acknowledgment and independent review. Staff see only their own/assigned items. Policies/training require a version and acknowledgment before final review.
- Offboarding approval blocks while open visits, assigned open tasks, attendance or future duties remain. An admin must review the completed checklist to disable a non-superuser account. Superuser offboarding remains a system-administrator action. This is account deactivation, not automatic transfer of all clinical work or deletion of history.
- Budgets and expense requests are explicitly **UGX**. Another supervisor approves them. Overlapping approved budgets for the same facility/cost-centre and approvals exceeding the budget are rejected. Supplier/payee and supporting invoice reference are retained; duplicate references within the same budget/payee are rejected. Approval records an obligation, not a bank or cash payment. These figures are not labelled profit. Uploaded supporting-document storage, supplier settlement and accounting export remain future work.

## Upgrade and verification

Back up database/private media, pull the release, run `python manage.py migrate` then `python manage.py collectstatic --noinput`. Operations migrations 0019–0022 add workforce/history and managerial registers. Migration 0020 refreshes canonical-patient guards for duty assignments. No staff attendance or credentials are fabricated from historical logins.

Assign staff to a facility and department before creating duties. Use separate requester/reviewer accounts. Start with a synthetic roster and review actual clinic policy before using attendance exports. Staff acceptance, physical devices and live-provider commissioning are independent gates.

Automated tests cover shift/leave conflicts, stale revisions, inactive staff, overnight recurrence, own clocking, retry protection, breaks, correction history, independent review, accepted cover, patient assignment/capacity, handover ownership, HR privacy and facility scope. Manager regressions cover case closure, expense limits, asset service history, checklist acknowledgment and offboarding guards. PostgreSQL tests contend overlapping publication and concurrent clock-in. Browser checks include staff/manager pages across 390/768/1440 widths, automated WCAG rules and a real clock-in/break/resume/clock-out form journey.

## Remaining work, not implied by these screens

Configurable staffing minima, formal employment lifecycle fields, shift-group publication, atomic reciprocal swaps, facility grace/rounding rules, unattended clock-out correction, escalation notifications, granular approval limits/delegations, document attachments, supplier settlement, full financial profitability and service-quality KPI definitions remain explicit extensions. Portal/engagement, provider interfaces, MFA/enterprise setup and clinically governed specialty scope remain in R3–R5. External providers, clinical rules and facility acceptance require their actual specifications and authorized owners.
