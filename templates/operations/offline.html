Use a private, managed device with an operating-system screen lock. Your passphrase encrypts the downloaded context and drafts; it is never sent to the server. A forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered.

Financial transactions, stock posting, result release and booking confirmations require a connected workspace.

3. Review and synchronize

Only reviewed drafts are sent. Conflicts require fresh context and a manual review. If a network response is lost, retry the same submission to check its outcome without creating a duplicate.

Local device storage

Browser data clearing can erase unsynchronized work. Device revocation blocks future synchronization; it cannot remotely erase data on a disconnected device.
